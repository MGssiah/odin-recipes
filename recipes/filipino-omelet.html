Filipino Omelet

Description

A Filipino version of the usual omelet recipes with simple addition

Ingredients

  • 3 Eggs
  • 2 Tomatoes
  • Onions
  • Vinegar
  • Magic Sarap MSG
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Beat the three eggs in a small bowl until properly mixed
  2. Add a dash of salt, msg and a table spoon of vinegar in the whisked eggs
  3. Cut Onions and Tomatoes into smaller pieces
  4. Saute onions in a heated pan in oil until soft and aromatic
  5. Add cut tomatoes until slightly seared
  6. Add the whisked egss in the pan
  7. Make sure to let the mixture spread on the pan
  8. Wait until golden brown and flip the omelet
  9. Wait again and flip back to other side
  10. Fold one side of the omelet and serve in a plate
