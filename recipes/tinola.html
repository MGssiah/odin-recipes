Tinola

Description

Everyday Filipino Food that is easy to prepare

It involves boiling chicken in various vegetables and fruits

Ingredients

  • Chicken cuts
  • Salt
  • Garlic
  • Chayote
  • Ginger
  • Malunggay
  • Onions

Steps

  1. Mince garlic, ginger and onions
  2. Cut Chayote in smaller pieces
  3. In a heated pot, saute garlic, onion and ginger until onions are softened produce a sweet aroma
  4. Add the chicken cuts into the pot and sear until golden
  5. Add a tablespoon of Salt
  6. Add 2 cups of water
  7. Wait until water boils and let it simmer for 10 minutes
  8. After 10 minutes, add the chayote wedges into the pot.
  9. Continue to simmer for 15-20 minutes
  10. Turn off stove and add malunggay leaves, cover the pot for 2 minutes
  11. Tranfers contents to a serving bowl
