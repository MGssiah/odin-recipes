Adobo

Description

A Filipino staple food usually prepared for both everyday life and festivities

It involves braising pork cuts in soy sauce for rich flavors

Ingredients

  • Pork cuts
  • Soy sauce
  • Garlic
  • Bay Leaves
  • Vinegar
  • Black pepercorn

Steps

  1. Cut garlic into small pieces
  2. Branch in oil until light brown
  3. Sear pork cuts in the oil with the garlic until golden brown
  4. Add 2 cups of water
  5. Add 2 to 3 Bay leaves
  6. Add Vinegar
  7. Add Black pepercorn
  8. Let it boil until 3/4 of water has evaporated
  9. Serve in a ceramic bowl
